SETUP: SYNC YOUR HOUSEHOLD DATA THROUGH GOOGLE DRIVE (one time, about 5 minutes)
================================================================================
Household data (attendance, payments, people, journal, planner) syncs between your devices through YOUR own Google Drive,
using a small script that runs in YOUR Google account. Nobody else can read it.
This is a DIFFERENT script from the Money one (money-backup.gs). Make a separate Apps Script project for each,
with a different secret key and a different web app address. Never paste one's address into the other.

FIRST-TIME SETUP
1. Open https://script.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
2. Click "New project". Delete the sample code.
3. Open the file household-sync.gs (in this repository), copy ALL of it and paste it into the editor.
4. Near the top, replace  CHANGE-THIS-TO-YOUR-OWN-HOUSEHOLD-KEY  with a long secret phrase of your own.
   Keep it private. You will type the same phrase in the app.
5. Click Save. At the top choose the function "authorizeOnce" and click Run.
   Google asks you to allow access to Drive: Review permissions > choose your account >
   Advanced > "Go to (project name) (unsafe)" > Allow.
   (It says "unsafe" only because you wrote the script yourself and Google has not reviewed it.)
6. Click Deploy > New deployment. Click the gear icon > "Web app".
      Execute as:         Me
      Who has access:     Anyone
   Click Deploy. Copy the "Web app" address. It must start with https://script.google.com/macros/s/ and end with /exec
7. CHECK IT: paste that address into a browser. You should see
   "Help Register HOUSEHOLD script is running (version h2)."
   If you see a Google sign-in page or an error instead, go to TROUBLESHOOTING below.
8. In the app: Settings > Household > "Sync across devices". Paste the address and your secret key,
   press "Save and sync". The status at the top should change to "Synced".
9. Repeat step 8 on every device with the SAME address and key.
   If you already have household data on several devices, sync first from the device with the most complete data.
   The others then merge into it (the newest change wins; deletions are remembered).

UPDATING THE SCRIPT LATER
Replace the code, keep your SAME key, then Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil (Edit) > Version: "New version" > Deploy.
The address stays the same. Without "New version" the old code keeps running.

TROUBLESHOOTING
"Unexpected token '<'" or the app shows "Not synced" with a web page error
  1. Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil > Version: New version > Deploy.
  2. "Who has access" must be Anyone.
  3. Use the address ending in /exec (not the editor address, not "/dev", and not the Money script address).
  4. Open the address in a browser: it must show the "running" message without asking you to sign in.
"Wrong key"  The key in the app is not exactly the same as the KEY line in the script (check for spaces).
"Not synced - busy, will retry"  Another device saved at the same moment. Press Sync again.

DRIVE FILES
The script keeps its data in the Drive folder "Help Register Household Sync": household-latest.json, plus one safety copy
per day (household-YYYY-MM-DD.json) for 30 days.

SECURITY NOTES
* "Anyone" only means the address can be called; every request must carry your secret key, otherwise it is refused.
  Do not share the address and key, and do not commit your real key to GitHub.
