SETUP: BACK UP YOUR MONEY DATA TO GOOGLE DRIVE (one time, about 5 minutes)
==========================================================================
Your expense data is saved into YOUR own Google Drive by a small script that runs in YOUR Google account.
Nobody else can read it. Do this once, then enter the address and key in the app on each device.

1. Open https://script.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
2. Click "New project". Delete the sample code.
3. Open the file money-backup.gs (in this repository), copy ALL of it and paste it into the editor.
4. Near the top, replace  CHANGE-THIS-TO-YOUR-OWN-SECRET-KEY  with a long secret phrase of your own
   (for example 4 random words and some digits). Keep it private. You will type the same phrase in the app.
5. Click the Save icon. At the top choose the function "authorizeOnce" and click Run.
   Google asks you to allow access to Drive: Review permissions > choose your account >
   Advanced > "Go to (project name) (unsafe)" > Allow.
   (It says "unsafe" only because you wrote the script yourself and Google has not reviewed it.)
6. Click Deploy > New deployment. Click the gear icon > "Web app".
      Execute as:         Me
      Who has access:     Anyone
   Click Deploy. Copy the "Web app" address (it starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/ and ends with /exec).
7. In the app: Money > Settings > "Back up to Google Drive".
   Paste the address and your secret key, tick "Back up automatically", press "Save settings",
   then "Test connection" (it should say Connected), then "Back up now".
8. Check Google Drive: a folder "Help Register Money Backup" now holds money-latest.json and one copy per day.

RESTORE: on a new phone or computer, enter the same address and key, then press
"Restore: replace everything on this device" (or "add missing records" to merge).

NOTES
* "Anyone" only means the address can be called; every request must carry your secret key, otherwise it is refused.
  Do not share the address and key.
* If you ever change the script code, use Deploy > Manage deployments > edit > New version.
* To use a different secret key later: change KEY in the script, create a new version, and update the app.
